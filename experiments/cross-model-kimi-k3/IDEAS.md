# IDEAS.md — Stage 1 Divergent Matrix (MODEL_LABEL = kimi-k3)

Raw premise matrix. Every premise was written **world-first** and had to pass the
**Naked Toy Test**: *if all code/programming UI were stripped away, would this
still be an intrinsically fascinating toy or predicament?*

Persistent protagonist used across all demos: **Pip** — a small teal round
explorer with huge eyes, a glowing antenna bulb, and orange feet (inline SVG,
readable at 48px).

---

## Vector A — Environmental Law Mutation

1. **Gravity Dial Hollow** — A float-pod with fixed buoyancy sits in a vertical
   hollow. Hanging moss bands at three heights are thirsty. The only control is
   the hollow's gravity *law over time*. A pod that rockets past or naps on the
   ground teaches the law-writer something.
2. **The Rain Bell Roof** — Bronze bells on a rooftop set the cloud-condensation
   rule above a garden. The garden wants exactly three showers in a week; a
   fourth bell-pattern brings a flood that floats the seed trays away.
3. **Moonwell Tide Clock** — A tide-pool village of shellfish beds needs a
   wet/dry *rhythm*, not a water level. Turning the moon-dial rewrites the tide
   period; get the period wrong and beds either drown or bake.
4. **The Slow-Light Greenhouse** — A dial controls how fast dawn arrives.
   Seedlings only unfurl if light crosses a threshold *while* the night-chill
   still lingers. Rush the dawn and they stay shut; crawl it and frost wins.

## Vector B — Asynchronous Delegation & Helpers

5. **Firefly Lamplighter** — A night garden of dormant glow-mushrooms. A firefly
   friend will fly any patrol you hum to it — looping forever. Mushrooms only
   charge while the firefly hovers glowing nearby. A bad loop dances in an empty
   corner while the garden sleeps.
6. **Bee Waggle Translator** — You write the waggle-dance route a scout bee
   broadcasts; the *hive squad* then flies it, not you. Orchards bloom only
   where the squad actually lingers.
7. **The Apprentice Golem's Morning** — A clay golem repeats whatever morning
   routine you demonstrate once — including your mistakes, forever. The mill
   needs sails turned *before* the grain hopper opens, or flour floods.
8. **Sheepwhistle** — Compose whistle phrases a sheepdog interprets to move
   cloud-sheep off a cliff meadow before the storm. The dog is smart; your
   phrases are not.

## Vector C — Temporal & State Echoes

9. **Echo Twin Waltz** — A stone door needs two floor plates held at the same
   time. Your movement plan is also executed by a golden echo of yourself,
   offset by D seconds. You are both the dancer and the choreography.
10. **Seedpod Domino Slope** — Pods on a hillside burst a fixed fuse-time after
    being nudged. Nudge order/timing decides whether the rolling nut reaches the
    squirrel door or plops into the pond.
11. **Drum Echo Canyon** — Sound takes seconds to cross the canyon. Three
    door-drums must ring *together*; you strike one drum now and schedule the
    echo-bounces to arrive in unison.
12. **The Sleepwalker Lullaby** — Pip sleepwalks at midnight along a ridge. You
    pre-record a lullaby path during the day; at night it plays back and steers
    her. A lullaby recorded near the cliff edge is... instructive.

## Vector D — Sensor-Actuator Networks

13. **Mole Sensor Greenhouse** — Four soil beds hide their moisture. Two sensor
    moles can be posted to any two beds and report honest numbers; the other
    beds are mysteries you can only schedule blindly or infer. Overwatering
    breeds mold; underwatering crisps.
14. **Cricket Thermometer** — Crickets chirp faster when warm. Vent a glasshouse
    by reading chirp-*rate*, not temperature. Orchids faint above a band; cold
    draft below another.
15. **The Listening Lanterns** — Lantern moths bump into shapes in a dark cave
    and report what they hit. Their reports are your only picture of the cave;
    doors open when the right silhouette is confirmed nearby.

## Vector E — Emergent Ecological / Flux Systems

16. **The Ladybug Ledger** — A rose bush, an aphid colony that grows
    logistically, and a lantern holding a *budget* of ladybugs. Choose release
    days/counts. Release everything early: aphids crash, ladybugs starve, and
    the day-10 winged aphid cloud finds a predator-free buffet.
17. **Millpond Gatekeeper** — Dam gates feed a mill wheel. A storm week is
    coming; too little head and the mill stops, too much and the village floods.
    You are scheduling pressure, not moving water yourself.
18. **Compost Heartbeat** — Microbes heat a compost heap when fed greens and
    browns in rhythm. Keep the heap in the warm band through cold nights by
    scheduling feedings; smother it and it goes sour.
19. **Lanternfish Schooling** — Keep a fish school dense enough that the
    anglerfish stays calm, sparse enough that everyone eats. Density is a moving
    equilibrium you nudge with current-gates.

## Vector F — State Metamorphosis & Wiring

20. **The Prism Burrow** — A beam of ember-red light must arrive at a gate as
    gold. Three transformer flowers each rewrite the beam's color by their own
    law (Ember swaps red↔blue, Mint swaps blue↔gold, Sun swaps red↔gold).
    Socket order *is* the program. The beam shows you exactly where it went
    wrong.
21. **Cocoon Loom** — Caterpillars metamorphose only in the chamber matching
    their current stage (heat → mist → dark). Route each crawler between
    chambers as its state changes; a cocoon in the mist-chamber just sogs.
22. **The Crystal Choir** — A door opens when three crystals hum the same note.
    Striking one crystal propagates its note along wires you lay; notes shift at
    junctions. Convergence, not volume, is the goal.

## Structural Pruning Log (aggressive rejections)

- **Wishing-Well Bucket Brigade** — rejected: delivery pipeline; protagonist as
  courier violates the Causal Anchor axiom.
- **Bat Sonar Dark Cave** — rejected: navigation maze wearing a sensor skin.
- **Bridge Plank Sorting** — rejected: disguised sorting-algorithm worksheet.
- **Golem Mail Route** — rejected: courier loop, errand-runner trope.
- **Password Rune Door** — rejected: guess-the-author's-sequence; no living
  system underneath.
- **Any "this level teaches loops" framing** — rejected on principle (axiom 1).
