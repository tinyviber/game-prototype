Cross-Model Blind Exploration

MODEL_LABEL: claude-sonnet-5 — six independently-designed programming puzzle prototypes, each drawing from a distinct systemic archetype. See IDEAS.md and DIRECTIONS.md for the full ideation and selection process.
DEMO 01

🌱 Gravity Well Gardener

Author a timeline of gravity-direction changes; a vine grows one cell per tick along the current law, bending around rocks only when the law itself changes.

Vector A · Law Mutation
DEMO 02

🪖 Windup Sentries

Program a repeating patrol loop, wind up the soldier, and step back completely while a known crow schedule tries to slip past.

Vector B · Delegation
DEMO 03

⏳ Echo Chamber Bridge

Two parallel programs — your recorded Echo and your live self — race on one shared clock. The gate opens only if both press on the identical tick.

Vector C · Temporal Echoes
DEMO 04

🦇 Blind Cave Fish Sensor Network

You can never see the cave-bear directly — only a motion sensor. Wire NOT/AND logic correctly before you trust the exit door.

Vector D · Sensor Networks
DEMO 05

🪷 Pond Algae Equilibrium

Set a standing feeding rule for a nutrient valve and watch 24 simulated days unfold — success means staying balanced, not reaching a finish line.

Vector E · Flux Systems
DEMO 06

🤖 Circuit Golem Innards

Open the golem's chest and rewire its crossed eye-to-arm connections — you're debugging its own body, not commanding it from outside.

Vector F · State Wiring
