Blind Exploration Batch · ox-alpha

Six single-file prototypes probing how programming can emerge from world systems rather than curriculum. One persistent protagonist — Tilo — across all six. Each starts with an intuitively plausible but flawed setup: press the button and debug the world.
VECTOR A · LAW MUTATION

The Leaning Grove

A vine grows toward whichever sun-orb sings brightest. You cannot touch it — you may only retune the song of light, breath by breath, until it crosses the chasm to the Mother Bloom.

 VECTOR B · DELEGATION

Norbert's Night Round

Pin cards onto a sleepwalker's dream. At dusk he executes it alone: past the landslide, around the geese — and someone must deal with the cat on the lever before dawn.

 VECTOR C · TEMPORAL ECHOES

The Convergence Bells

Five bells, five wavefronts, one shrine that wakes only for a perfect chord. Schedule the striking so every tone lands on the same beat — one bell hides in slow mist.

 VECTOR D · SENSOR NETWORKS

The Fog Chimes

A moonmoth crosses fog along a forgotten path. Plant chimes that ring when she passes, lamps that answer — and read the golden breadcrumbs your own light leaves behind.

 VECTOR E · FLUX & EQUILIBRIUM

The Mill Weir

Rain, drizzle, drought. Write the pond's two standing rules and keep seventy turns on the wheel before dusk — without drowning the beavers' hallway.

 VECTOR F · WIRING & METAMORPHOSIS

The Spore Telegraph

The chant-totem hums red·blue·blue·red·blue. The door dreams blue·blue·red. Rewire the fungal switchboard and spend Tilo's two graft-spores wisely.
