The Mill Weir

Rains come, droughts come. The old mill needs 70 turns of the wheel before dusk; the beavers need their hallway dry. Write the pond's two standing rules — then let the weather argue with them.

Pond Rules — evaluated every heartbeat, top to bottom

The miller's old note says: “open early, the wheel must always turn.”
The headgate feeds the wheel (needs a good splash to count as a turn). The shut board leaks a thin trickle that still counts.
If the pond sits past the red line while the gate is shut for a few heartbeats, the burrow hall floods. The sky-strip up top is tomorrow's promise — read it.
